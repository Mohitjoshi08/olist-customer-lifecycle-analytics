# Business Insights

### Revenue
**Observation:** GMV peaked in November.
**Evidence:** 34% increase compared to annual monthly average.
**Business Interpretation:** Elevated demand in November indicates opportunities for seasonal inventory management.
**Recommendation:** Prepare marketing/inventory for November.

### Retention
**Observation:** Low repeat purchase rate.
**Evidence:** 3.0% repeat rate.
**Business Interpretation:** Data indicates that acquisition volume is significantly higher than repeat purchase volume, suggesting retention as an opportunity for sustainable growth.
**Recommendation:** Launch loyalty campaigns for first-time buyers.

### Geography
**Observation:** Revenue concentration.
**Evidence:** Top 5 states drive 68% of revenue.
**Business Interpretation:** Revenue is concentrated in specific states, which is associated with regional dependency and logistics risk.
**Recommendation:** Targeted regional expansion and logistics hardening.

### Products
**Observation:** Product concentration.
**Evidence:** Furniture/Bed Bath drive 27% revenue.
**Business Interpretation:** High revenue concentration in these categories indicates they are major revenue contributors and may represent opportunities for cross-selling.
**Recommendation:** Focus on product bundles.

### Delivery
**Observation:** Late delivery impact.
**Evidence:** 8.11% late delivery rate.
**Business Interpretation:** Logistics issues correlate with a higher detractor rate in customer reviews.
**Recommendation:** Prioritize logistics optimization.

### Customer Reviews
**Observation:** High detractor potential with late deliveries.
**Evidence:** Review scores are associated with delivery performance.
**Business Interpretation:** Timely delivery correlates with higher customer satisfaction.
**Recommendation:** Improve delivery tracking communication.

### Sellers
**Observation:** Seller concentration.
**Evidence:** Top 1% sellers generate 18% GMV.
**Business Interpretation:** High reliance on a small number of sellers may create operational dependency.
**Recommendation:** Develop a more diversified seller network.

### Customer Value
**Observation:** Low CLV for most.
**Evidence:** Median CLV R$ 107.78.
**Business Interpretation:** Revenue is concentrated, indicating high opportunity for segment-based growth strategies.
**Recommendation:** Implement high-value customer loyalty program.
