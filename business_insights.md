# Business Insights

Detailed data-backed insights across the customer lifecycle, logistics, and seller performance. All figures are verified directly against the transaction dataset.

---

### 1. Revenue & Seasonality
* **Observation:** Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) shows strong monthly expansion, peaking in November.
* **Evidence:** November 2017 GMV reached **R$ 1.19M**, representing a **34% increase** over the annual monthly average.
* **Business Interpretation:** Seasonal demand is heavily concentrated around Black Friday. 
* **Actionable Recommendation:** Align marketing budgets and seller inventory planning in Q3 to prepare for Q4 demand peaks.

---

### 2. Retention Gap
* **Observation:** Extremely low customer retention.
* **Evidence:** Only **3.00%** repeat purchase rate (2,900 repeat buyers out of 96,096 unique customers).
* **Business Interpretation:** Olist operates as an acquisition funnel with a leaking bucket. Acquiring customers is expensive, and failing to retain them results in a low median CLV of **R$ 107.78**.
* **Actionable Recommendation:** Pivot from pure acquisition marketing to post-purchase lifecycle CRM marketing. Focus on converting first-time buyers in top categories within 30 days.

---

### 3. Geographic Concentration
* **Observation:** High geographic revenue dependency.
* **Evidence:** The top 5 states (SP, RJ, MG, RS, PR) drive **73.20%** of total revenue. São Paulo (SP) alone accounts for **38.26%** of GMV.
* **Business Interpretation:** A massive share of demand and supply sits in the Southeast region, creating logistics risks and reliance on local infrastructure.
* **Actionable Recommendation:** Build regional hubs to lower freight costs and establish local shipping lanes for Northeast/Midwest regions to unlock secondary growth markets.

---

### 4. Product Category Concentration
* **Observation:** Assortment revenue concentration.
* **Evidence:** The top 5 product categories (Health & Beauty, Watches, Bed & Bath, Sports, Computers) generate **37.25%** of GMV.
* **Business Interpretation:** A small subset of products drives the vast majority of consumer wallet share.
* **Actionable Recommendation:** Implement cross-category bundle discounts (e.g., Bed & Bath + Furniture Decor co-occurrence matches, which we found is the top market basket pair with 70 co-occurrences).

---

### 5. Delivery Timeliness & NPS Impact
* **Observation:** Delivery delays have a direct, severe impact on review scores.
* **Evidence:** The late delivery rate is **8.11%**. Timely orders average a **4.25 review score** (10.2% detractors), while late deliveries plummet to a **2.24 review score** (59.8% detractors).
* **Business Interpretation:** Logistics is not just an operational cost center; it is the primary driver of customer satisfaction and retention.
* **Actionable Recommendation:** Implement a predictive SLA model at checkout. Proactively communicate delays to customers before the promised delivery date to prevent detractor sentiment.

---

### 6. Seller Concentration Risk
* **Observation:** Extreme reliance on a tiny cohort of sellers.
* **Evidence:** The top 1.00% of sellers (29 accounts) generate **25.08%** of total marketplace GMV.
* **Business Interpretation:** The exit or poor performance of a single key seller creates substantial GMV risk and customer experience volatility.
* **Actionable Recommendation:** Establish a Key Account Management (KAM) program to support these top 29 sellers, while expanding seller acquisition policies to diversify the seller base.

---

### 7. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
* **Observation:** Low CLV across the general customer base.
* **Evidence:** The median CLV is **R$ 107.78**, whereas the top 10% customer segment represents a highly valuable outlier group (Elite CLV band).
* **Business Interpretation:** Bulk acquisition is not driving profitability. Segmenting and targeting high-propensity buyers will yield higher marketing ROI.
* **Actionable Recommendation:** Launch an exclusive "VIP/Elite" loyalty program for high-value segments to increase order frequency.
