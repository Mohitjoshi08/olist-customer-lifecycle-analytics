# Project Charter

## Project
Customer Lifecycle Analytics

## Business Problem
The business has historical e-commerce transaction data but lacks a structured understanding of customer retention, purchasing behavior, and long-term customer value. As a result, opportunities to improve retention and revenue are difficult to identify.

## Business Objective
Analyze the complete customer lifecycle to identify opportunities for improving customer retention, customer lifetime value (CLV), and overall business performance through data-driven insights.

## Dataset Summary

| Metric | Value |
|---------|------:|
| Orders | 99,441 |
| Customers | 96,096 |
| Products | 32,951 |
| Sellers | 3,095 |
| Review Period | 2016–2018 |

## Success Metrics (KPIs)

- Total Revenue
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Customer Churn Rate
- On-Time Delivery Rate
- Average Review Score

## Deliverables

- SQL business analysis
- Python exploratory and advanced analytics
- Power BI dashboard
- Business recommendations
