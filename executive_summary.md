# Executive Summary

## Dataset
- 99,441 Orders
- 96,096 Customers

## Problem
Although the marketplace served 96,096 unique customers, only 3.0% placed another order.

## 5 Key Findings
1. Repeat purchase rate is only 3.0%.
2. Late delivery rate is 8.11%.
3. Top 5 states drive 68% of revenue.
4. Median CLV is R$ 107.78.
5. Delivery delays are associated with customer detractor status.

## 5 Recommendations
1. Implement post-purchase loyalty campaigns.
2. Optimize logistics for high-delay regions.
3. Launch high-value customer program.
4. Enhance cross-sell strategies for top product categories.
5. Improve SLA monitoring for critical shipping lanes.

## Business Impact
- Retention optimization
- Logistics cost reduction
- Improved high-value customer targeting
- Increased revenue through personalized strategies.

## Expected Business Value
- Improve repeat purchases
- Reduce logistics inefficiencies
- Improve marketing ROI
- Support executive decision making
